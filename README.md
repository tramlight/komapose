# KomaPose

A lightweight, single-file 2.5D anime drawing reference doll built for everyday slice-of-life poses.

[**▶ Open KomaPose in your browser**](https://tramlight.github.io/komapose/)
*(Or download `index.html` and open it locally — runs 100% offline with zero dependencies.)*

## Why?

Most 3D posing apps are overkill for casual standing, sitting, and dialogue shots—they often have distracting hyper-detailed faces, awkward shoulder/hip mesh deformation, and cluttered lighting setups. KomaPose trades extreme action poses for fast controls, simple guidelines you can actually sketch over, and consistent proportions.

## Features

* **8-Angle Turntable \& Twist:** Rotate the whole rig in 45° steps (`←` / `→` or `\[` / `]`) or twist the pelvis, ribcage, and head independently.
* **3D Local Joints + 2D Linework:** Ball-and-socket shoulders/hips paired with 1-DOF hinge elbows and knees, anatomical joint limits, and color-coded foreshortening cuffs.
* **Quick-Swap Hand Stamps:** 8 everyday gestures (Relaxed, Fist, Open Palm, Peace, Chin Rest, Hold Phone, Hold Cup, Hold Pen) across 4 camera-resolved views.
* **Flexible Proportions \& Neutral Faces:** Scale from chibi (`2.2 heads`) to tall (`8.0 heads`), adjust build width/softness, and switch between Crosshair, Simple, and Planes face guides.
* **Desk \& Seat Guides:** Toggleable, tiltable reference lines to ground sitting, leaning, and lying poses.
* **Sketch-Ready Export:** One-click transparent PNG export with an optional **Blue Sketch Lines** mode for dropping straight under your drawing layer.

## Quick Controls

* **Drag joint dots:** Pose limbs directly on canvas
* **Mouse wheel over a limb:** Adjust foreshortening depth / hinge bend
* **Drag empty canvas / `Space + Drag` / Pinch:** Pan \& zoom
* **`←` / `→` (or `\[` / `]`):** Rotate turntable ±45°
* **`M`:** Mirror pose Left ↔ Right
* **`Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y`:** Undo / Redo
* **`H`:** Hide all UI overlays

## Note

This is a rough-and-ready sketching aid rather than a polished 3D renderer—you'll occasionally see contour breaks or overlap quirks at certain angles. It's meant to get proportions and spatial rhythm on the page quickly so you can draw over it in your own style.
